# Responsible AI in EngagePulse

EngagePulse predicts potential student disengagement using
early learning activity and machine learning.

## Principles

- Predictions support academic decisions, not replace them.
- High-risk predictions require human review.
- Low engagement does not necessarily indicate low motivation.
- Model performance must be evaluated across student groups.
- Predictions must not determine grades or student eligibility.
- Personal student information must be protected.
- Model limitations and uncertainty must be communicated clearly.

## Objective

Develop transparent, fair and explainable student-risk
predictions that help universities provide timely support.
