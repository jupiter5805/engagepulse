# Student Engagement Research Hypotheses

EngagePulse investigates the relationship between early
learning behaviour and subsequent academic outcomes.

## Hypotheses

- H1: Lower learning activity is associated with higher academic risk.
- H2: Declining weekly engagement predicts adverse outcomes.
- H3: Consistent learning activity improves risk prediction.
- H4: Behavioural trends provide more predictive value than total activity alone.

## Validation Approach

- Analyse historical OULAD student engagement data.
- Compare individual feature contributions.
- Evaluate hypotheses using held-out course presentations.
- Distinguish statistical associations from causal relationships.

These hypotheses require empirical validation before
being translated into intervention recommendations.
