# Student Engagement Intervention Strategy

EngagePulse aims to identify students who may benefit
from additional academic support.

## From Prediction to Action

1. Identify students with elevated predicted risk.
2. Review engagement trends and model explanations.
3. Allow academic staff to assess individual circumstances.
4. Offer appropriate, supportive interventions.
5. Monitor outcomes and evaluate intervention effectiveness.

## Possible Interventions

- Academic tutoring and study support
- Personalised engagement check-ins
- Learning resource recommendations
- Referral to relevant student support services

## Measuring Success

Evaluate intervention outcomes using:
- Student retention rates
- Changes in engagement patterns
- Academic progression
- Student feedback

Predicted risk alone does not establish that an
intervention is necessary or effective.

## Research Objective

Connect predictive analytics with measurable student
outcomes through responsible, human-led interventions.
