# Interpreting Student Risk Predictions

EngagePulse estimates the likelihood of a student experiencing
an adverse academic outcome based on early engagement data.

## Understanding Predictions

- A risk score is a probability estimate, not a certainty.
- Higher scores indicate greater predicted risk.
- Predictions are based on patterns in historical student data.
- Low activity alone does not prove disengagement.
- Model explanations identify associations, not causes.

## Using Predictions Responsibly

Academic staff should consider additional context before
deciding whether a student may benefit from support.

EngagePulse should be used for early intervention research,
not automated academic decisions.

## Model Limitations

Predictions may be affected by incomplete data, changes
in student behaviour and differences between institutions.
Performance should be monitored and periodically re-evaluated.
